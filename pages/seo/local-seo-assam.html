FAQ

Common SEO questions from Assam business owners

Mere competitor pahle se Google par hain — kya hum unhe haraenge?
Assam ke most markets mein competition abhi bahut kam hai. 2-3 months ke consistent SEO se top 3 mein aana possible hai — especially Guwahati ke outside cities mein.
SEO aur paid Google Ads mein kya fark hai?
Google Ads mein aap pay karte ho per click — band karo to traffic zero. SEO mein ek baar rank karo to organic traffic free milta hai. SEO slower hai but long-term mein far cheaper aur more trustworthy.
Website nahi hai toh kya SEO ho sakta hai?
Google Business Profile ke liye website mandatory nahi. Lekin website hone se ranking bahut better hoti hai. Hum website + SEO bundle mein discount bhi dete hain.
